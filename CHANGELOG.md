# Changelog

## v0.1 (beta)

- All 62,227 translatable strings in English: story and stage scripts, battle quotes, unit /
  pilot / weapon names, menus and system messages, database pages, executable strings and
  terrain names. AI-assisted first pass, checked for fit, line breaks and consistent names.
- Proportional English: a patch to the text layout and measuring routines draws 1-byte
  characters with the game's own Latin glyphs; umlauts and accents are composed into spare
  font cells.
- Name glossary of 1,793 names (`glossary/impact_names.tsv`) and settled terms
  (`glossary/impact_terms.tsv`).
- 105 stage title cards redrawn in English; episode numbers read "EP. N" and "FINAL".
- Opening and ending story crawls (25 pages) and the three part title cards redrawn in English.
- Battle screen: command badges, support / skill labels and the 24 series banners in English.
- Title logo fly-in with English lettering; staff credits (35 pages) romanized and translated.
- Distributed as an xdelta patch against the Limited Edition ISO.
